# KitchenForge × Marathon Hardware
### A workflow-native channel for the cabinet shops Marathon already serves.

---

## ~$1.0M ARR in Year 1 through the Marathon channel — and a multiple of that in hardware pull-through.

KitchenForge is a SaaS productization of Southbrook Cabinetry's working Odoo 19 platform (live at `southbrookcabinetry.space`). The Marathon integration is already scaffolded: partner record, 20 finishes, 5 knob templates, hardware resolver, CSV import wizard. We're not pitching a roadmap. We're pitching a live system with the Marathon hooks already in the code.

---

### For Marathon

- **Demand signal before the PO.** Telemetry on every Marathon-spec'd cabinet — SKU, finish, shop, project size — landing in Marathon BI in real time.
- **Structural presence in the spec workflow.** Marathon's catalog is the default hardware library on every channel shop's quoting screen. Override is opt-in; most shops won't.
- **Auto-RFQ to the nearest branch.** Threshold-triggered RFQs land in branch inboxes with the full BOM attached.
- **6-month category exclusivity** locks Richelieu and Häfele out of the workflow lane while Marathon defines it.

### For cabinet shops

- **45–90 minutes back per kitchen.** Hardware specified once, propagated to quote, cut-list, and PO automatically.
- **Fewer callbacks.** Exclusion rules encode 15 years of Southbrook shop-floor decisions — wrong-hinge, wrong-slide errors caught at spec time.
- **AI-agent surface.** Describe a kitchen in plain English; get a quoted, hardware-specced project.
- **$149/month/shop**, no hardware up-charge, no platform lock-in beyond a 30-day notice.

### For the relationship

- **60/40 subscription split** (Southbrook / Marathon). Predictable recurring revenue both sides recognize quarterly.
- **$8 USD rebate per Marathon-spec'd cabinet** flowing on a transparent ledger, reconciled monthly.
- **Co-branded launch, joint sales-ops review weekly for 90 days.** One executive sponsor each side.
- **Pilot cohort of 10 Marathon top accounts** onboarded on the house in the first 30 days — the test we both pass or fail together.

---

The 12-month conservative case is 280 shops, ~38 cabinets/shop/month, ~$1.0M ARR through this channel. The aggressive case is roughly 2.5x that. But the number Marathon should track is hardware pull-through per spec'd cabinet — every kitchen in the system originates inside Marathon's catalog, and we have a year of production data from Southbrook to underwrite that assumption. The platform exists, the integration is scaffolded, and the only thing standing between this brief and a paying channel SKU is a 14-day decision and a catalog handoff under NDA.
